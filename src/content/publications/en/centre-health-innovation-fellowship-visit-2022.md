## The visit at Chalermbhrakiat Building

HealthTAG joined the Centre for Health Innovation Fellowship visit on 14 December 2022 at Chalermbhrakiat Building, Siriraj Hospital. The team thanked Siriraj Hospital for including HealthTAG in the programme.

Participants explored HealthTAG's work and exchanged ideas about access to health information across borders. HealthTAG said it looked forward to continuing the collaboration after the visit.
