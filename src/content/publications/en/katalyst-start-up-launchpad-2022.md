## Eight weeks of focused business development

Thank you to KATALYST Start-up Launchpad 2022 for giving the HealthTAG team the opportunity to join its exclusive and intensive eight-week business workshop.

We entered the programme ready to learn and improve together, then use that experience to help health innovation and practical progress reach people across Thailand. At the programme's conclusion, HealthTAG received the second runner-up result.
