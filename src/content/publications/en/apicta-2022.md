## The result published by APICTA

The APICTA 2022 winner list names Healthtag of Thailand as the Cross Category: Start-Up winner. The listed entry is "BLOCKCHAIN-BASED PHR & HEALTH DATA MANAGEMENT PLATFORM."

The source confirms the competition result and product name. It does not certify the system, record regulatory approval, or establish a healthcare deployment.

## Blockchain in the current HealthTAG system

In the current HealthTAG architecture, hospitals control clinical records. Blockchain records consent and access events for audit. Clinical records are not stored on blockchain.
