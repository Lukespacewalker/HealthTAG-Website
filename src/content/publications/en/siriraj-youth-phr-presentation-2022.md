## The Siriraj World Class 5G Smart Hospital event

HealthTAG joined the Siriraj World Class 5G Smart Hospital event on 25 August 2022 at Siriraj Medical Research Center. The team presented Permission Based Blockchain for PHR to students from Prasarnmit Demonstration School.

The students learned how blockchain can be applied in medicine. The session gave them an introduction to concepts that can inform the development of health innovation in Thailand.
