## The presentation at Siriraj Medical Research Center

On 5 September 2022, HealthTAG presented its blockchain-based personal health record project to visitors from Singapore's Tan Tock Seng Hospital at Siriraj Medical Research Center.

The presentation began with the problem of health information being held in separate systems, then introduced HealthTAG's approach to connected and decentralized care. HealthTAG described its goal of helping patients access and take part in managing their personal health information anytime and anywhere, while hospitals remain the custodians of clinical records.
