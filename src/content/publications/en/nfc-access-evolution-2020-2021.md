## Early concepts

A post from 14 April 2020 records an early NFC concept for opening a health web page without installing an app. A post from 10 May 2021 then records an explored Self-Isolation Tracking Wristband, without establishing a release or deployment.

## Current use

HealthTAG confirms that the team later moved to an NFC sticker. The sticker is one entry point to the PHR and does not store the user's clinical record.
