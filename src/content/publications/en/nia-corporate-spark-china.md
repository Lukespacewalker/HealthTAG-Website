## See you in Shanghai

HealthTAG's original announcement records a first runner-up result in the selection for the Corporate SPARK Outbound 2025 China programme organised by Thailand's National Innovation Agency. NIA later named HealthTAG as one of three Thai companies joining the programme's China activities.

This was an important step in taking HealthTAG's health innovation into conversations with the Chinese market. The programme included business matching and market development support, creating opportunities to learn from regional partners and explore future growth in China and beyond.
